# E13: Go-to-market & partnerships (ongoing)

Goal: reach 100 paying shops and about €10,000 ARR by 31 Dec 2027. Web checker, content and listing are in E9; this epic covers partners, communities and tracking.

## E13-S1: Recruit design partner agencies

As the Invoqk team, we want 5 to 10 German WooCommerce agencies testing early, so that we get feedback and referrals.

- Outreach list and pitch ready; each partner gets a free Agency plan.
- Feedback sessions logged.

## E13-S2: Recruit Steuerberater partners

As the Invoqk team, we want 2 to 3 tax advisors recommending the plugin, so that merchants trust it.

- Advisors review the export format (E7-S6).
- Referral link and materials provided.

## E13-S3: Run an affiliate program

As a partner, I want a 30% commission on referred sales, so that recommending Invoqk pays off.

- Tracked links through the payment provider (E8) or own tracking.
- Monthly payout process documented.

## E13-S4: Show up in the communities

As the Invoqk team, we want regular presence in the places our users are, so that we get installs.

- Posts or talks in WooCommerce DACH Facebook groups, WordCamp or WP Meetup DE, r/woocommerce and German e-commerce newsletters.
- A simple calendar of planned activities.

## E13-S5: Cross-sell to existing users

As the Invoqk team, we want to tell users of the Invoqk web app, the Sheets add-on and Twinspot merchants in DACH, so that we reach warm leads.

- Announcement emails and in-app notices.

## E13-S6: Track the success metrics

As the Invoqk team, we want a simple dashboard of key numbers, so that we can decide at each gate.

- Active installs, trial starts, free-to-paid conversion, paying shops, ARR, renewal rate, agency accounts, time to first report, tickets per paying shop.
- Reviewed monthly against the 2027 targets.

## E13-S7: Run support

As a paying shop owner, I want fast email support, so that problems do not block my invoices.

- Support inbox with templates; agency plan gets priority.
- Target under 0.3 tickets per paying shop per month.
